# Privacy

**Short version: WaqtWise keeps everything on your computer. It collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- **Program privacy statement.** This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.
- **No network.** The app does not connect to the internet. Prayer times are calculated on your computer. There is
  no update check, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account.
- **Where your data is.** Your tasks, settings and statistics are stored in files in the app's data folder on your
  computer: `%APPDATA%\WaqtWise` (`data.json` and `state.json`, plus a `backups` folder). Nothing else leaves it.
- **Location.** The app does not ask Windows for your location. You choose your country and city yourself (or type
  coordinates); they are stored only in the data file, and are used only to calculate prayer times. Browser
  permissions such as location, camera and microphone are refused by the app.
- **Backups you export** are normal files that you create and keep. Anyone who has the file can read it, so
  store it where you trust.
- **Removing everything.** Uninstall WaqtWise and choose to delete its data, or delete the folder above.
- **This website and this repository** are hosted by GitHub. GitHub may log visits according to its own
  [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  The website of this project has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, fonts or images. Its
  "What's new" box asks GitHub's public API for the latest release when you open the page.
- **Downloading.** Releases are downloaded from GitHub, which sees the download like any other.
