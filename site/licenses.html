Third-party software and data used by WaqtWise

WaqtWise is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 or later (see LICENSE and NOTICE.md). The parts below are made by other people and keep their own licenses. All of them allow use inside a GPL program.

Part What it is used for License
Electron The program that runs WaqtWise as a Windows app (it includes Chromium and Node.js; their license texts are installed with the app) MIT (Electron), BSD-style and others (Chromium, Node.js)
adhan Works out the five prayer times from the city's coordinates MIT
Plus Jakarta Sans (through the npm package @fontsource/plus-jakarta-sans) The font of English text and digits SIL Open Font License 1.1
IBM Plex Sans Arabic The font of Arabic text (files and license in src/renderer/fonts-arabic/) SIL Open Font License 1.1
GeoNames Names, regions, coordinates and population of about 30,000 cities (src/core/world-cities.json, see src/core/WORLD_CITIES_SOURCE.md) Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 - attribution is given here and in the app's website
IANA time zone database Which time zone each city is in (the file zone.tab was used to build the city list) Public domain
electron-builder Builds the Windows installer (a development tool, not part of the app) MIT

Attribution required by CC BY 4.0: City data from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cities were filtered by population, their time zones were assigned from the IANA database, and the data was reduced to the fields the app needs.
