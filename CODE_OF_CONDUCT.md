# Code of conduct

We want WaqtWise to be a friendly place for everyone, in English or Arabic, whatever your experience, country or beliefs.

**Do**
- Be kind and patient. People come with different levels of experience.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you need.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.

**Don't**
- Insult, harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
- Share other people's private information.
- Post spam or off-topic promotions.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block
people who repeat it. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the item, or contact the maintainers
through a private security report (Security tab).

This code applies to the issue tracker, discussions, pull requests and any space that represents the project.
